## ◈ PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Mobile Ocean Shop Management System** is a digital management solution designed to simplify and organize mobile shop operations.

The system aims to bring essential business activities into one platform, helping shop administrators manage products, monitor stock, process orders, maintain customer information and track sales efficiently.

Built with a focus on usability, organization and scalability.

## ◈ CORE FEATURES

### Product Management
- Add, edit and delete mobile products.
- Maintain product information and specifications.
- Organize products by categories.
- Manage product pricing.

### Inventory Management
- Monitor available stock.
- Update stock quantities.
- Track inventory changes.
- Identify low-stock products.

### Order Management
- Create and manage customer orders.
- Track order statuses.
- View order details.
- Maintain order records.

### Customer Management
- Store customer information.
- Manage customer records.
- View customer purchase history.

### Sales & Reports
- Monitor sales transactions.
- Review business performance.
- Track revenue and sales records.

### Admin Dashboard
- Centralized management interface.
- Quick business overview.
- Organized navigation.
- Easy access to management features.
